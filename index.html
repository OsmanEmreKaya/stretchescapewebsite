One cat. One line. Every tile.

A cozy grid puzzle where a stretchy cat follows your finger, leaving a trail behind. Cover every square without getting stuck — 120 hand-made levels, and only one solution each.

Three ways to play

One puzzle rule, three very different rhythms — a calm journey, a nerve-racking sprint, and a single shared puzzle each day.

Story

120 levels, six islands

Take your time. Levels grow from four-by-four warm-ups into knots that need a plan. Fish and yarn hidden in the grid pay out coins, and every level scores up to three stars.

Escape Run

Solve fast or the run ends

An endless mode against a shrinking clock: each solved board stretches your streak, one slow board ends it. Best streaks land on the Game Center leaderboard, so you can chase your friends.

Daily Puzzle

The same board for everyone

One tough level a day, identical for every player, on a timer. You get one try — and one more if you want it. Miss it and a fresh puzzle arrives tomorrow.

Six worlds to unlock

Every twenty levels the scenery changes and a new island appears on the map — from a green valley to a city built around a castle.

Spend your coins

Coins come from the fish and yarn you find in Story levels and from Daily Puzzle rewards — never from your wallet. There is nothing to buy with real money in Stretch Escape.

Costumes, themes and boards

Dress the cat, recolor the trail it leaves behind, and swap the board it walks on. Everything in the shop is earned by playing.

Plays anywhere, anytime

No account, no internet needed to solve a level. Sound, music, vibration, hints and the on-screen timer are all switches you can turn off — and a daily reminder you can turn on.

Plays in your language

Stretch Escape picks up your device language on first launch, and you can change it any time from Settings.
