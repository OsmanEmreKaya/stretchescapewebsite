Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This policy explains what Stretch Escape (“the game”, “we”) does with data when you play it. The game is made and published by Our Games Studios.

The short version: we don’t ask for your name, your email or an account, and we don’t sell anything about you. Your progress stays on your device. A small amount of anonymous usage and advertising data is processed by Google so we can keep the game free and see which parts of it people actually play.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, shop purchases, best streak and your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, hints, reminders) are stored in the app’s own storage on your device. We cannot see them, and they are not uploaded to us. Deleting the game deletes them.

What is collected, and by whom

  • Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics). Anonymous events about how the game is used — which screens are opened, when a level is started or finished, shop and ad outcomes — plus general technical information such as device model, operating system version, country and language. This is not linked to your identity and is not used to identify you.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob). The game shows ads, and AdMob processes data needed to deliver and measure them, including approximate location derived from your IP address and coarse device information. Stretch Escape does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA), so ads are contextual rather than based on tracking you across other apps and websites.
  • Game Center (Apple). If you are signed in to Game Center, your best Escape Run streak is submitted to Apple’s leaderboard, where your Game Center nickname and photo are visible to other players. This is handled entirely by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy; we never receive your Apple Account details.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health data or payment information. The game has no user accounts and no in-app purchases.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No push server is involved and nothing about it leaves your phone. You can turn it off in the game’s settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Your choices about ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your consent choices about personalised advertising the first time the game runs. You can reopen that dialog and change your answer at any time from the game’s Settings screen.

On iOS you can also limit ad measurement system-wide in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising and Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Third parties

The services above are provided by Google and Apple, who act as independent controllers of the data they collect. Their own policies apply:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in AdMob
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Data processed by these services may be transferred to and stored on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their policies.

Children

There is nothing in Stretch Escape a young child shouldn’t see: the App Store content rating is 4+, and a small child can happily play it. That rating describes the content of the game.

Privacy law asks a different question — who the game is aimed at. Because Stretch Escape shows third-party ads and uses analytics, it is published as a general-audience game rather than a child-directed one: it is not listed in the App Store Kids Category and it is not targeted at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, there is normally nothing for us to look up or delete. To erase everything the game stores, use the reset option in the game’s settings or delete the app. For data held by Google or Apple, please use the links above. If you are in the EEA or the UK you have rights of access, erasure, restriction and objection under the GDPR; write to us and we will help you direct the request to the right place.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date above. Meaningful changes will also be noted in the game’s release notes.
